About

Dual Intelligence LLC

Dual Intelligence LLC is a technology company that develops AI-assisted tools and services to support personalized planning in areas such as beauty, wellness, and cognitive well-being. We combine intelligent software with real-world professional context — a practitioner's experience, a research literature, a person's own history — to build practical, user-centric tools rather than generic AI output.

Structure

What we are

  • A registered Idaho LLC, operating as a software / technology company
  • Parent company for a portfolio of applied AI products, starting with Vibrant Intel
  • Focused on personalized planning and decision support, not treatment delivery

What we're not

  • Not a healthcare, medical, or clinical wellness provider
  • Not a diagnostic or treatment system of any kind
  • Not in the business of generic, one-size-fits-all AI advice

How we work

Three commitments that shape every product we build under Dual Intelligence LLC.

Research-driven

Recommendations trace back to a specific source or rule — not an unexplained model output.

Safety-first

Screening against a person's actual current medications, supplements, or history happens before any suggestion is generated, not after.

Plainly scoped

Every product states clearly what it is — and isn't — including where it stops short of medical advice.
