Where the Framework Is Actually Being Built

Four areas where we're applying the human-plus-AI approach described on the Vision page. Each one is labeled honestly by status — live, in development, or exploratory — because a credible roadmap says what isn't finished yet, too.

In Development

Health, Beauty & Wellness — Vibrant Intel

Personalized beauty and wellness planning connected to real professional services: body and facial massage, scalp therapy, foot reflexology, eyebrow tattoo, hyaluronic acid filler, and beauty/health consultation. The AI's role is planning and organization — matching a client's goals and history to a coherent plan — not diagnosis or treatment.

Live

Cognitive & Mental Well-Being — Cognitive Daily

A personalized nootropic and supplement recommendation engine. A customer describes their goals, current medications, and current supplements; the system cross-references a research-derived knowledge base and screens explicitly for drug interactions before producing a report — compound, dose, timing, and the reasoning behind each one.
